Privacy Policy

App Name: AzeEng Crossword
Developer: LearnAppLanguage
Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LearnAppLanguage ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the AzeEng Crossword mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number directly through the App.

The following types of data may be collected:

  • Device Information: Device identifiers and advertising IDs used by third-party advertising services (Google AdMob).
  • Game Progress: Your game progress (levels completed, hint count, score) is stored locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Sharing Tokens: If you use the "Share" feature, a temporary anonymous token is generated and stored in our Supabase database to verify your sharing action. No personal information is linked to this token.
  • Usage Data: Basic usage statistics may be collected by third-party services integrated into the App (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used for the following purposes:

  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To verify sharing actions and reward users with additional levels and hints.
  • To improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • To maintain and operate the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase — used as a backend service to manage sharing verification tokens.
    Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies — the game engine used to build the App. Unity may collect certain technical data.
    Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

5. Data Storage and Security

Game progress data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. Sharing tokens stored in Supabase are anonymous and do not contain any personally identifiable information. We take reasonable measures to protect the data associated with the App.

6. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

  • The right to access the data we hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion of your data.
  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.
